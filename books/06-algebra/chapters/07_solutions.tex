\chapter{Fully Worked Solutions}
\label{ch:solutions}

\section{Solutions of the doctoral problems}

Solutions are written up problem by problem, in the order of
\cref{ch:problems}. Many of the imported papers carry an official or
reconstructed solution in the source database; those are being edited into the
house style before they are added here, so that every solution in this volume is
complete rather than a sketch.

Nothing in this chapter is generated automatically from the problem import: the
import writes only to \texttt{chapters/06\_doctoral\_problems.tex}.
