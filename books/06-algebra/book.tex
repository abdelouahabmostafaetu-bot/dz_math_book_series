\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}
\markboth{Preface}{Preface}

This volume collects the algebra of the Algerian doctoral entrance examinations:
groups, rings, fields, modules, Galois theory, algebraic number theory, finite
fields, order and lattice theory, and the algebraic side of coding and
cryptography.

The problems are taken verbatim from the papers actually sat at Algerian
universities, and they are kept in French, the language in which they were
written. They are grouped by university and ordered by year, so that a reader
preparing for a particular \emph{concours} can work through the house style of
that university first.

A warning about the sources is in order. Examination papers are filed by a single
subject label, and that label is often wrong: a paper labelled \emph{Algèbre} may
consist entirely of Fourier transforms, or of graph colouring, or of symplectic
geometry. Each problem in this volume was therefore classified on its own
mathematical content rather than on the label of the paper it came from. The rule
used is written down in \texttt{docs/PROBLEM\_ROUTING.md}; problems that turned
out not to be algebra were sent to the volume where they belong.

\bigskip\noindent\emph{Abdelouahab Mostafa}\\
